Build planning guide

Plan a 2007 Chevrolet Tahoe Street Build

Use BDK as real inspiration, then organize your own wheels, lighting, exterior, and audio priorities.

Start with the direction, then identify exact parts

BDK combines 26-inch wheels, window tint, refreshed headlights and taillights, Texas Edition trim, and three Memphis subs in a custom ported box. Those submitted details show the direction of the project; they do not provide tire dimensions, offsets, amplifier specifications, or exact part numbers.

Build your research list

  1. Record your Tahoe's year, trim, drivetrain, engine, and current modifications.
  2. For wheels, ask the supplier to confirm the complete wheel-and-tire fitment against your exact vehicle and suspension setup.
  3. For audio, get an installation plan covering enclosure design, wiring, protection, and electrical requirements.
  4. For lighting and exterior parts, identify exact assemblies, connections, and local use requirements before ordering.

Separate your budget into four buckets

BucketWhat to include
PartsMain products plus required supporting hardware
InstallationShop labor, fabrication, and setup
Other purchase costsShipping, taxes, and supplies from your actual quotes
ContingencyA reserve you choose for unexpected work

Compare two directions

Save your plan to My Garage, duplicate it, and name the versions Daily Tahoe and Show Tahoe. Change one category at a time, then use Compare Builds to see the cost difference. Keep your real quotes in the workbook rather than treating the planner's estimate as a vendor price.
