Budgeting a Vehicle LED Refresh

Make a lighting inventory and quote the complete upgrade before choosing a look.

List each lighting location

Separate headlights, taillights, interior lamps, and decorative lighting. Record what is currently installed and whether you are replacing a bulb, an assembly, or adding a new circuit.

Identify the exact application

Give the supplier your year, make, model, trim, and existing lighting equipment. Ask for the exact product application and installation instructions. A product's appearance or a general LED label does not establish compatibility.

Collect a complete quote

  • Main lamps or assemblies
  • Required adapters, controllers, wiring, and protection
  • Installation and setup
  • Any additional supporting work identified by the installer

Keep road use and decorative use separate

Confirm that the exact lighting setup is suitable for its intended use and location before purchase. Ask the supplier and installer to identify any use restrictions that apply to the proposed parts.
