# First shop partnership pilot

Draft proposal. No shop contacted; no paid listing sold.

Choose one service area and one relevant shop category. Ask the owner for named businesses before sending outreach.

## Pilot deliverables
- Accurate profile: shop name, location/service area, services, business contact, website.
- Completed work photos supplied with publication permission.
- Clearly labeled sponsorship if payment or another material connection exists.
- Direct visitor contact route chosen by the shop.
- A small measurement report: profile visits and outbound inquiries, with no invented lead counts.

Do not forward visitor contact information to multiple shops. A future quote form must name the recipient and let the visitor explicitly choose to send it.

## Outreach draft
Subject: BuildMyBeast shop partnership conversation

Hi [shop contact],

I run BuildMyBeast, a free vehicle build planner and member gallery. We're exploring a small partnership with a [specialty] shop serving [area].

Would you be interested in a conversation about a shop profile featuring your services and completed builds? We would agree on scope and any pricing before publication, and paid placements would be labeled.

You can see the site at https://buildmybeast.com/ and partnership information at https://buildmybeast.com/partners/.

Thank you,
BuildMyBeast
contact@buildmybeast.com

## Decide before charging
Price; placement duration; asset requirements; review process; reporting; cancellation/refund handling; no guaranteed traffic or lead volume.
