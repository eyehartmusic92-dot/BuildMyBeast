How BuildMyBeast parts research works

1. Your configuration narrows the research. Year, make, model and available engine/trim/drivetrain details are included where useful.

2. Your selected upgrade sets the shopping intent. Wheel, suspension, audio, performance and other searches are built around the upgrade level you chose.

3. Marketplace results are not automatic fitment approval. Listings and seller data can change. Always confirm the exact part number and manufacturer fitment before ordering.

    03 — About BuildMyBeast

    From build idea to smarter parts research.

    BuildMyBeast is an independent automotive build-planning and product-discovery platform created for enthusiasts modifying trucks, SUVs, cars, off-road rigs, performance vehicles and custom projects.

    🛠️

    Plan before buying

    Select a vehicle, build style, target budget and upgrade categories to organize a project before ordering parts. Parts and labor figures are planning estimates, not quotes.

    🔎

    Vehicle-aware research

    BuildMyBeast uses year, make, model and available configuration details to make product research more relevant. Exact fitment still needs to be confirmed with the manufacturer or retailer.

    🚘

    Visualize the direction

    Upload a vehicle photo to generate an AI concept from the selected build plan. Concepts help explore a direction; they are not engineering drawings or guarantees of real-world fitment.

    How recommendations are handled

    BuildMyBeast separates planning guidance from verified product fitment. A product should only be presented as verified when reliable product and vehicle-fitment information supports that claim. Retailer or affiliate relationships may influence where a purchase link points, but they do not remove fitment warnings or turn an unverified product into a verified one.

    Affiliate Disclosure

    BuildMyBeast may use affiliate links to automotive retailers and brands. If you purchase through an eligible affiliate link, BuildMyBeast may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not change our fitment warnings or the need to verify product compatibility before purchase.

    Privacy

    BuildMyBeast does not sell personal information. The site uses Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and site interactions. These analytics services may process information such as page views, interactions, browser or device information, referring pages and approximate geographic information. If you join Free Build Alerts, your email address is submitted to Kit, the email service used to manage subscribers and email updates. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link provided in those emails. When you use the AI vehicle visualizer, the photo you choose and the build instructions are sent to the site's AI image service for processing. Only upload images you have permission to use. Gallery submissions are checked by Cloudflare Turnstile and sent to Cloudinary for manual review. Approved photos and build details may be displayed publicly after approval.

    Contact

    For questions, business inquiries or partnerships, email contact@buildmybeast.com.
